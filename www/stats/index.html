Training plan

How it adapts

The next 7 days are on the device under Workouts, listed first. They're rebuilt each time you open this app: harder when you're fresh, easier when you're tired, VO2max and sprint targets from your own best efforts. Workouts you edit yourself are kept as they are.

Riders

The active rider is the one the device rides with: FTP sets ERG targets, Max ERG caps them, weight is for W/kg. Changes are saved on the device right away (not during a ride).

Firmware update

First tap Wi-Fi → Allow firmware update on the device, then choose the firmware file within 5 minutes: justride-<version>.bin from a release (not the -full.bin, that one is for USB), or build/justride.bin.
